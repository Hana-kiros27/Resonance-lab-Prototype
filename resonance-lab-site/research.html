Our Research

The RESONANCE AI4D Lab conducts cutting-edge research focused on developing ethical, scalable, and contextually relevant AI solutions to address Ethiopia's most pressing development challenges.

Innovative Health Solutions

Developing AI-powered tools to improve healthcare access and outcomes, particularly in rural and underserved areas.

  • AI Telemedicine for Rural Health: AI-powered telemedicine platform to bridge rural healthcare gaps.
  • AI Diagnostics for Early Detection: AI diagnostic tools for early detection of diseases, including non-communicable diseases and maternal health issues.
  • Predictive Analytics for Outbreak Management: Predictive analytics for outbreak monitoring and management (e.g., malaria, antimicrobial resistance).

Resilient Agriculture and Food Systems

Leveraging AI and IoT to enhance food security, improve productivity, and mitigate climate variability in agriculture.

  • Precision Agriculture and Resource Optimization: AI-driven precision farming tools for optimizing resource use (e.g., water, fertilizer).
  • AI-Driven Dairy Management: IoT-based dairy management systems to monitor herd health, milk quality, and cold chain management.
  • AI for Wheat Disease Management: Develop AI models using UAV images to identify and manage wheat rust and other diseases.

Inclusive Governance and Justice

Enhancing transparency, optimizing decision-making, and improving public service delivery through AI.

  • AI-Driven Judicial Case Management: AI tools for judicial case management to streamline processes and reduce backlog.
  • Natural Language Processing and Resource Allocation: AI-based public resource allocation systems for transparent and efficient governance.
  • Sentiment Analysis for Policy Feedback: Sentiment analysis tools for monitoring public feedback and improving policy-making.

Sustainable Energy and Climate Resilience

Designing AI-powered models to optimize energy forecasting, promote sustainable energy, and build climate resilience.

  • Energy Demand Forecasting: AI-powered forecasting models for predicting energy demand and renewable energy supply.
  • AI for Energy Distribution: Optimization tools for energy distribution to underserved and off-grid areas.
  • Climate Risk Prediction and Early Warning System: AI-powered early warning system integrating satellite imagery, meteorological data, and real-time weather analysis.
