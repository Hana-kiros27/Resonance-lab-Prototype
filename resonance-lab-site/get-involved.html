Opportunities for Students

We are building a robust and inclusive AI talent pipeline. Join our Masters or PhD research positions, or align your ongoing research with our core focus areas.

Masters and PhD Research Positions

  • Academic Qualification: MSc or equivalent degree in a relevant field (minimum CGPA applies).
  • Technical Prerequisites: Proficiency in programming, statistics, or domain expertise.
  • Application Requirements: Motivation Letter, Recommendation Letters, optional Written Assessment, Interview.
  • Inclusion Priority: Strong encouragement for women and candidates from marginalized communities.

Check our News & Events section for application deadlines!

Research Participation for Currently Enrolled Students

Currently enrolled Masters and PhD students at AAU can align their ongoing research with our lab's thematic areas.

  • Eligibility: Must be currently enrolled, research aligned with lab themes, written support from supervisor, willingness for interdisciplinary collaboration, commitment to Responsible AI principles.
    • Masters Eligibility: To be eligible for a Masters position, you must be a current AAU Masters student who has successfully completed their first year of study and is in good academic standing.
    • PhD Eligibility: To be eligible for a PhD position, you must meet one of the following criteria:
      • You are a current AAU PhD student who has successfully completed their first year of study and is in good academic standing.
      • You are an incoming PhD student who has taken and passed the Graduate Admission Test (GAT).
  • Application: 2-page concept note, letter of endorsement from supervisor, 1-page motivation statement.

Benefits of Participation for Students

Access to Lab Infrastructure

Computing resources, software tools, IoT kits, and curated datasets.

Supervision & Mentorship

Guidance from domain experts from health, agriculture, governance, and energy sectors.

Monthly Research Stipend

Reasonable monthly stipend is provided to students so they can focus on their research.

Funding for Research Activities

Support for data collection, fieldwork, or prototyping (subject to approval).

Publication & Conference Opportunities

Opportunities to co-author publications and present at conferences.

Priority Access to Events

Lab-hosted workshops, training, hackathons, and regional AI4D exchange programs.

Official Recognition

Affiliated researcher status, enhancing academic and professional credentials.

Collaborate & Partner with Us

We actively seek strategic collaborations with academic institutions, government entities, industry partners, and community organizations to enhance our impact and ensure the relevance, scalability, and sustainability of our initiatives.

Areas of Collaboration

  • Joint research projects and co-creation of AI solutions.
  • Faculty and student exchanges with other AI4D labs (Kenya, Tanzania, Rwanda, Ghana).
  • Resource sharing and capacity building initiatives.
  • Policy development and advocacy for ethical AI governance.
  • Commercialization of research outputs and startup incubation.
  • Community outreach and stakeholder engagement.

If your organization is interested in partnering with the RESONANCE AI4D Lab, please reach out to discuss potential synergies.
