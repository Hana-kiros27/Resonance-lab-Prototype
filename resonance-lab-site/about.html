Our Mission

The Resonance Lab (Responsible AI Solutions and Networks for Sustainable Development) at Addis Ababa University aims to position Ethiopia as a leader in Responsible AI for development. We focus on creating ethical and scalable AI solutions tailored to Ethiopia's specific needs, advancing key Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) across health, agriculture, governance, and energy.

Background and Context

Ethiopia, a diverse country in the Horn of Africa, faces critical challenges in achieving sustainable development. With over 85% of its population reliant on agriculture, the sector is highly vulnerable to climate variability and inefficiencies. The country also struggles with limited access to quality healthcare, energy reliability, and governance transparency.

Ethiopia's 10-Year Development Plan (2021-2030) aligns with the United Nations' SDGs. Artificial Intelligence offers an unprecedented opportunity to overcome these challenges by providing scalable and contextually relevant solutions. However, Ethiopia faces a severe shortage of trained AI professionals, limited access to high-performance computing infrastructure, and siloed research efforts.

In response, the RESONANCE AI4D Lab seeks to establish Ethiopia as a leader in Responsible AI, addressing critical national and regional issues through ethical, scalable, and inclusive AI applications.

Our Objectives

Develop Ethical and Scalable AI Solutions

Design and implement AI solutions to tackle Ethiopia's critical challenges in health, agriculture, governance, and energy & climate.

Build a Robust and Inclusive AI Talent Pipeline

Implement strategies to ensure a sustainable and inclusive pipeline of AI talent in Ethiopia, fostering interdisciplinary research and global perspectives.

Strengthen Regional and International Collaboration

Enhance Ethiopia's AI research ecosystem through partnerships with local universities, government entities, private sector stakeholders, and other AI4D labs in Africa.

Shape Evidence-Based AI Policy and Governance Practices

Develop policy recommendations and guidelines to support ethical and effective AI integration aligned with national development priorities and SDGs.
